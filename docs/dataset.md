# Initial Human Dataset

## Dataset

MindTrace will initially use behavioral data from the Penn Electrophysiology of Encoding and Retrieval Study (PEERS), collected by the Computational Memory Lab at the University of Pennsylvania.

## Initial Experiment

We will begin by investigating PEERS Experiment 1.

This experiment includes human participants performing free-recall memory tasks.

## Why We Are Using It

The dataset gives us real human recall behavior that we can later compare with language models.

Instead of running our own human experiment, we can first reproduce known patterns from existing experimental data.

## First Goal

Before involving any AI model, we want to answer:

**Can we load the human recall data and reproduce one basic pattern of human memory?**

Our first target will likely be the **serial-position effect**: whether people remember words differently depending on where those words appeared in the original list.

## Important

At this stage:

- We are not training a model.
- We are not prompting an LLM.
- We are not trying to improve AI memory.
- We are first understanding the human data.
