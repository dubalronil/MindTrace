# First Research Question

## Question

Do language models remember information in ways that resemble human memory?

## First Focus

We will start with a simple memory task:

- Humans are shown a list of words and later asked to recall them.
- A language model is given a similar list and asked to recall it.
- We compare the patterns in what humans and the model remember.

## What We Want to Learn

We are not asking whether an AI has human memory.

We are asking whether its **observable recall behavior** shows some of the same patterns found in humans.

For example:

- Are items at the beginning or end remembered differently?
- Are related words recalled together?
- Are words that appeared near each other recalled together?
- What kinds of mistakes are made?

## Current Scope

For the first experiment, we will study **free recall of word lists**.

We will not build a new AI memory system yet.
